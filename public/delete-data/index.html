Delete My Data

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data and your child's learning data from Pathways at any time. Fill in the form below and we will process your request within 30 days.

What data we hold

  • Account details — name, email address, phone number
  • Child profiles — name, grade, school
  • Learning progress — notes read, test scores, quiz history
  • Subscription and payment records
  • App usage analytics (anonymised)

Submit a deletion request

Request received ✓ We'll process your deletion request within 30 days and send a confirmation to your email address.

What happens next

We will acknowledge your request within 3 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been removed from our systems.

For urgent requests or any questions, email us directly at support@pathways.co.ke.
